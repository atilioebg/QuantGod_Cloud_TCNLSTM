## 🛠️ Key Bug Fixes & Improvements

During the stress test, several critical pathing and compatibility issues were identified and patched to ensure a robust "Single Source of Truth" (SSOT) from `master_config.yaml`:

1.  **Dynamic Path Resolution:** Patched `split_dataset.py`, `experiment_utils.py`, and training scripts to resolve directories using `master_config.yaml` thresholds (e.g., `splits_labelled_...`) instead of hardcoded strings.
2.  **Scaler Compatibility:** Resolved an `UnpicklingError` in `auditor_labelling.py` by switching from `pickle` to `joblib` to match the `run_foundation.py` saving logic.
3.  **Specialization Split Fix:** Corrected `run_specialization.py` to target the specialized split folder (`splits_specialized_labelled_...`) generated by the ETL.
